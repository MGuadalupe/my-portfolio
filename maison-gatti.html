Maison Gatti · Product design lead

Boosting digital sales through customizable artisan chairs

A mobile-first app with an interactive configurator, so people can design the handwoven bistro chair they want instead of dropping off halfway.

Role
Product design lead
Client
Maison Gatti, French heritage brand
Platform
Mobile-first app
Website
maison-gatti.com (opens in a new tab)

The challenge

Maison Gatti needed to elevate their digital buying experience. Many users were dropping off right at the personalization step.

Our goal was to optimize the UX to boost digital sales and empower users to create the chair they truly want.

About Maison Gatti

Maison Gatti is a French heritage brand known for its iconic handwoven rattan bistro chairs.

From a product design perspective, it represents a timeless blend of craftsmanship, tradition, functional beauty and Parisian café culture. The app had to carry that feeling while making customization simple.

The solution

We designed a mobile-first app featuring an interactive chair configurator.

What people can do in the app

  • Choose a model
  • Pick seat and back patterns and fabrics
  • Change frame colors
  • See changes in real time
  • Save and share favorites
  • Request a quote directly from the app
