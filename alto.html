Alto · Product designer

Increasing investment in real estate

A virtual investment portfolio for Alto, a real estate startup in Geneva, so investors can explore projects and plan payments on their own instead of waiting for an agent.

Role
Product designer
Client
Alto, Geneva
Team
Product owner, 5 developers, QA lead
Deliverables
Web app and UI kit

The context

Alto has a portfolio of real estate investments across Europe, including hotel constructions and large renovation projects, and stands out for its tailored payment plans.

But the new opportunities that kept arising had a significant deficit of investors. Agents had to leave their daily tasks and go out to sell the projects themselves.

Indicator

Deficit of investment

The agents who used to track investments had to shift their focus to finding new investors, or persuading existing ones to invest in new projects.

The problem

The lack of investment didn’t come from projects being uninteresting or unprofitable, nor from agents selling them poorly.

Agents could only offer each possible investor a 30-minute meeting. Even when those meetings repeated, they happened within a short span of time, so investors never had enough time to consider an investment or build trust in a project.

The hypothetical solution

The core problem was a communication channel that relied entirely on agents.

So I proposed a new platform where investors could actively search for opportunities and customize their payment plans to fit their needs, with agents joining when the investor was ready.

Task flows and user flows

Two people use the platform: an agent who needs to book time with investors, and an investor who wants to apply on his own.

Frida, Alto agent

Arrange an appointment with a potential investor
  1. Logs in to Alto
  2. Opens the Opportunities section
  3. Finds the project she wants to offer her client
  4. Opens the project details
  5. Arranges an appointment with the investor

Christian, real estate investor

Apply for a potential investment
  1. Signs in to Alto
  2. Validates his account
  3. Chooses a project in Opportunities
  4. Asks to invest in that project
  5. Books an appointment with an agent to evaluate the investment

  • Sustainability
  • Vibrant colors
  • Nature

Gradients, rounded shapes, bold typography, color and glassmorphism.
